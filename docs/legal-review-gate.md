# Legal / Privacy / Disclosure Review Gate

The public legal pages describe the current static research portal and deliberately avoid asserting an unverified legal entity, licence, regulatory exemption, client relationship, or jurisdiction-specific status.

Before the portal is used for regulated services, client onboarding, subscriptions, paid products, investment recommendations, data collection, or account features, the relevant pages must be reviewed against the actual legal entity, operating jurisdictions, licences/registrations, data flows, vendors, retention rules, and client agreements.

## Change triggers

Legal/privacy/disclosure review is required before introducing:
- account registration or login;
- contact or subscription forms;
- analytics, advertising, behavioural tracking, or non-essential cookies;
- payment or billing;
- client-specific recommendations or portfolio information;
- order routing, transaction functionality, or brokerage integrations;
- paid research subscriptions;
- customer support systems that collect personal data;
- new external data processors;
- claims about licences, registration, regulatory status, client counts, AUM, performance, awards, or named personnel credentials.

## Repository rule

Code review cannot substitute for legal advice in a specific jurisdiction. The repository should contain only claims that can be supported by the site's actual implementation and verified organizational evidence.
